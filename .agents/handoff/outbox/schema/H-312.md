## Done

Three measurements in `scripts/ci/check-presets.mjs`, each recorded in [[sot:svgif/preset-registry]]:

- **A looping ambient preset is not an entrance.** `samplesEntrance` exempted an ambient loop only when it had a lens clip. A one-period drift, which the registry allows, was sampled as an entrance. A full-frame pattern measures the same at every offset, so eight backgrounds and `nature-water-wave` failed the entrance gap. The wrap sample still runs on every looping ambient preset. A trial that put the wave back on its 160-frame seam failed with `frame after the last frame differs from frame 0`.
- **Text presets are sampled on the light stage.** Their ink is `#1c1915` so that the text reaches 4.5:1 on white in the Studio check. That ink is within the 16-level tolerance of the `#141414` stage, so the text was never measured. Measured on `#f7f7f7`, 12 of the 13 failing text presets passed at once. `text-marquee` passed with its loop options. A trial `text-block` that lands at frame 8 fails both the band and the entrance gap there.
- **Light ink in the bind check.** `maskedPaint` painted the targeted nodes on `#f7f7f7`. A light-ink pattern changed no pixel there, so `bg-grid` `weight`, a stroke width, and `bg-hex` `size` read as unchanged. The SVG did change: the stroke width went from 1.5 to 3. When the light page shows no change, the targets are compared again on `#141414`.

Self-test updates:
- `filter-drop-shadow` became a 30-frame hover that returns to rest in H-306. It is now seeked with that duration and the rest exemption, and the 2x seek measures 19,908 px.
- The listed-number example moved from `viz-bar` `barCount` to `bg-grid` `weight`, because H-311 gave `barCount` a reason.

`unbound.json` corrections to H-311:
- `bg-hex` `size` follows `scale`. The self-test says so, and the measured bind passes, so its reason was removed.
- The registry says `start` is not exempt unless a camera preset lists it, so the `text-counter` `start` reason was removed.

`node scripts/ci/check-presets.mjs --self-test` passes. `check-schema.mjs` passes. With H-313, `--goldens` reports one line: `text-counter` `start has no bind`.
