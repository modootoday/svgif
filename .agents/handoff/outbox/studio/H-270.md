## Done

Thirteen looping object presets are in `presets/objects/`:

- cart: rolls back and forth, and the wheels turn by distance over radius.
- paper plane: glides round an ellipse, nose along its heading.
- bulb: the glow and rays brighten and fade.
- gear: two gears mesh. The big gear carries a crank and turns once per loop; the small gear has half the teeth and turns twice the other way.
- dice: three quarter turns per loop at a steady speed, a hop each turn, and a new face each turn.
- trophy: sparkles twinkle and drift up around the cup.
- ticket: floats and tilts.
- megaphone: sound arcs radiate from the mouth.
- bell: swings, and the clapper lags behind.
- pencil: writes a wavy line, an `attr.d` reveal with unwritten points on the tip, and the pencil follows the tip.
- brush: paints a swatch from the left while the brush follows its edge.
- food bounce: a pudding squashes and stretches on a fixed bottom edge.
- plant pot: the leaves sway on the stem.

Notes for the preset check:

- A shape with rotational symmetry paints the same at every angle. A pair of plain gears turning by one tooth per loop showed no motion between the mid frame and the last frame, so the big gear has a crank and turns a full turn.
- The paper plane and the gears turn more than 30 degrees per 2 frames at their fastest, so they are keyed every frame.
- Rotations that pass a whole period (gears, dice) are written from the frame number, so the keys never run backwards at the wrap.
