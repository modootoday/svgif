# Handoff H-267

## State

## Done

Thirteen vehicle presets are in `presets/objects/`: car, taxi, bus, truck, ambulance, train, bike, scooter, moto, plane, helicopter, rocket, and boat. Each drives in from the left (the rocket rises from below) and stops at the centre with a smoothstep speed profile.

The catalogue says the wheels turn with the ground, so each wheel hub turns by the distance travelled over its radius, keyed every frame. Smoothstep peaks at 1.5 times the mean speed, and the drive time grows until no wheel step exceeds 28 degrees: small wheels drive for longer. The hub has three holes so the turn reads. The road line sits at the wheel bottoms.

The plane spins its propeller, the helicopter flickers its rotor, the rocket flickers its flame, and the boat rocks on a static wave band. Body layers take `color`. Glass, tyres, hubs, the ambulance cross, the taxi sign, the waves, and the sail keep their own fills. The taxi defaults to `#e0b326` and the ambulance to `#e8e8e8`. The train carries `sizeReason: "per-frame wheel keys"`; its golden is 17 KB.

`check-presets.mjs --goldens` on the category reported nothing. The index check and `check-presets.mjs` pass. Coverage is 149 of 476, a gap of 327, counted on `main` plus this batch.

Raster review: in the first draft the vehicles were small and the wheels floated 3 px above the road. They are now 1.5 times larger and rest on it.
