## Done

Nine looping object presets are in `presets/objects/`:

- ball bounce: one bounce, a squash on contact, and a shadow that shrinks as the ball rises.
- balloon float: bobs and sways on its string.
- box: a parcel hops, squashes on landing, and settles.
- book: the cover swings open about the spine and closes.
- cup: two steam wisps rise and fade.
- chair: a rocking chair rocks on its runners.
- lamp: the light cone and bulb brighten and dim.
- analog clock: the minute hand turns once per loop. The hour hand holds still, because a hand that advanced through the loop would jump back at the wrap. The minute keys are unwrapped to 0 through 360 so the hand never runs backwards.
- digital clock: seven-segment `12:0d`, where the last digit counts 0 to 9 per loop with dim unlit segments, and the colon blinks.

They use the looping layer generator from the effects batches (H-262, H-263), now writing to any category. Layers in the bound color take `color`; details such as glass, steam, tape, hands, and the clock case keep their own fills.

Raster review: in the first draft the clock hands were near-black on the dark stage; they are now light grey.
