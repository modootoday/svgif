# Handoff H-330

## State

Started from `main` at `307d43c`. `Studio shell` failed once on #566 with `animation.webm frames did not change: 0 pixels`. A re-run passed. The owner asked for the fix.

## Done

- **Video frame race.** When ffmpeg is missing, as on the CI runner, the check seeks a `<video>` element and draws it to a canvas.
  - The cause: `seeked` can fire before the frame at the new time is ready to draw. On a busy machine the late picture then repeats the opening frame.
  - The fix: `grab()` registers `requestVideoFrameCallback` before the seek, and waits for it after `seeked`. A seek to the time already shown presents no new frame, so the wait is capped at 1 second. Without the callback, it waits 100 ms.
  - The log line now reports `frameCallbacks`.
- **Profile cleanup race.** Under the same load, one run passed every check and then exited 1. `rmSync` of the browser profile threw `ENOTEMPTY` while Chrome's child processes were still writing.
  - `stop()` now reports a profile it could not remove and keeps the run's result.
  - `stop()` runs in `finally`, and a failed check still sets the exit code in `catch`. No assertion changed.

Measured with ffmpeg hidden from `PATH`, so the browser decodes the video as in CI, and with 8 busy loops on 4 cores:

| Script | Runs | Exit 1 | Cause |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `main` | 6 | 1 | `frames did not change: 0 pixels`, `changed=0 earlyLight=0 lateLight=0`, as in CI |
| frame wait only | 10 | 1 | `ENOTEMPTY` in profile cleanup, after every check had passed. The video proof passed in all 10 |
| both fixes | 10 | 0 | — |

Without load, `main` passed 6 of 6. With ffmpeg on `PATH`, the fixed script passes.
