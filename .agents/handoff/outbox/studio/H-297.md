# Handoff H-297

## State

Started from `main`. Five `later` catalogue types reuse the v1 particle generator with `attr.d` tracks: `nature.cloud.morph`, `nature.snow.accumulate`, `nature.plant.grow`, `nature.vine.grow`, and `nature.aurora`.

## Done

- `nature-cloud-morph`: one cloud outline moves its points between three cloud forms while it drifts over its shadow. The outline is a union of circles cast on 40 rays, so every form has the same command list. The cloud takes `color`.
- `nature-snow-accumulate`: flakes fall, and a drift on the ground rises under them, by moving the drift outline's points. It plays once.
- `nature-plant-grow`: a stem rises, two pairs of leaves unfold along it, and a flower opens at the tip. It plays once.
- `nature-vine-grow`: a tapered vine draws itself along a curling path, its tip leading, and a leaf opens at each turn. It plays once.
- `nature-aurora`: three translucent curtains ripple, drift, and pulse over a dark ridge.

The catalogue maps snow to a mask height and the vine to a path trim. These presets move outline points instead, which reads the same and needs no clip or dash property.

The files are 12,592 to 29,341 bytes and the goldens 10,921 to 26,512 bytes. The vine and aurora goldens are over 16 KiB and carry a `sizeReason`.

`check-presets.mjs --goldens` on `nature` reported nothing for these five, and `check-presets.mjs` passes.

The visual review passed on rasters of frame 0, the mid frame, and the last frame, judged against [[sot:svgif/handoff]], plus three in-between samples. It found three problems, all fixed:
- The one-shot presets sampled their last frame as frame 0, so the plant's leaves and the vine's leaves vanished on the last frame.
- Leaves that had not opened yet showed as specks.
- A first cloud built from bumps on an ellipse read as a blob.
