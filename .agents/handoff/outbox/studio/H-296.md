# Handoff H-296

## State

Started from `main`. Five `later` catalogue types reuse the v1 particle generator: `fx.trail`, `fx.fireworks`, `fx.afterimage`, `fx.inkSplash`, and `fx.dissolve`.

## Done

- `fx-trail`: six dots follow a figure-eight, each a few frames behind the one before it and smaller, so the line reads as a comet tail.
- `fx-fireworks`: two shells rise, burst into eight sparks each, and the sparks fall and fade. The first shell takes `color`. The golden is over 16 KiB, so the preset carries a `sizeReason` (eighteen particles).
- `fx-afterimage`: a card slides across and back. Three copies trail behind it, and their opacity follows the card's speed, so the trail vanishes when the card turns.
- `fx-ink-splash`: a drop falls, hits the floor, and throws a blot and seven droplets that spread, settle, and fade.
- `fx-dissolve`: a card of sixteen tiles fades out tile by tile in a scattered order, the tiles lifting as they go, then fades back in.

The files are 6,038 to 31,302 bytes and the goldens 4,134 to 26,755 bytes.

`check-presets.mjs --goldens` on `effects` reported nothing for these five, and `check-presets.mjs` passes. The builder drops opacity tracks that do not change, so a first afterimage with fixed ghost opacities drew every ghost at full opacity on top of the card; the ghosts now fade with speed.

The visual review passed on rasters of frame 0, the mid frame, and the last frame, judged against [[sot:svgif/handoff]], plus three in-between samples.
