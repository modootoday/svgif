# Handoff H-303

## State

Started from `main`. H-299 said five `later` types needed a filter graph in the engine. They do not:
- The motion-preset schema accepts `filter.<primitive>.<attribute>` for any primitive and attribute.
- The SMIL backend writes each one onto that primitive, with primitives in the order their tracks first appear.

So `in`, `in2`, `result`, `type`, `numOctaves`, `values`, and `operator` can be set as one-key tracks. A graph can use each primitive once, which these five need.

## Done

- `filter-turbulence`: fractal noise feeds `feDisplacementMap`, which warps a card and its cut-out text lines. The warp swells and settles while the noise frequency shifts. `strength` scales the displacement.
- `filter-color-matrix`: a gradient badge with a star cut out of it runs its hues once around a `hueRotate` matrix while it bobs.
- `trans-dissolve` (24 frames, plays once): noise is mapped to alpha through a colour matrix, and its threshold moves, so the outgoing card breaks into grains and is gone. Then the card takes the incoming colour and window, and its grains fill back in. It starts and ends whole. `duration` retimes it.
- `bg-noise`: film grain over a faint wash. The grain re-seeds every three frames and drifts. The ink is at opacity 0.24 and the wash at 0.12, the same as `bg-halftone`.
- `bg-paper`: long fibres and fine tooth, from anisotropic noise, over a warm wash, drifting slowly over a four-second loop. The ink is at opacity 0.2 and the wash at 0.12.

The files are 3,424 to 4,077 bytes, and every golden is under 2.1 KiB.

`check-presets.mjs --goldens` on `pathfx`, `transitions`, and `backgrounds` reported nothing for these five, and `check-presets.mjs` passes. `bg.noise` and `bg.paper` are not on the ambient list in `schema/fixtures/preset/motion-class.json`. They pass the mid-frame band instead.

The visual review rendered frame 0, the mid frame, the last frame, and in-between samples, each time on its own page, and judged them against [[sot:svgif/handoff]]. Problems it found, all fixed:
- A first dissolve was gone by frame 9, and it started and ended with holes.
- The drifting grain left an uncovered strip at the right edge, so the grain layer is now wider than the frame.
- The first paper fibres were dark and heavy.
- The badge star was not cut out, because both outlines wound the same way.
