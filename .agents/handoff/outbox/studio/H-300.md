# Handoff H-300

## State

Started from `main`. Four `later` catalogue types build on v1 presets:
- `viz.radar`, `object.drink.pour`, and `trans.morphMatch` use the v1 particle generator with `attr.d` tracks.
- `camera.handheld` reuses the `camera-shake` scene and its pose binds.

## Done

- `viz-radar`: a six-axis radar chart holds one series, moves its shape and its six points to a second series, holds, and moves back. The shape takes `color`.
- `object-drink-pour` (plays once): a bottle tips over a glass. It keeps tipping as it empties while a stream runs from its mouth and the glass fills, and then it is set upright.
- `trans-morph-match` (plays once): an app tile becomes an avatar. The same 32 points move from a rounded square to a circle while the shape travels and changes colour, and a name line opens under it.
- `camera-handheld`: the camera drifts on two slow, unrelated curves per axis, like a held camera, over a loop. It keeps the `distance`, `latitude`, and `azimuth` binds of `camera-shake`. It drops `strength` and `seed`, which `camera-shake` lists as unbound.

The files are 2,925 to 19,768 bytes and the goldens 9,874 to 17,523 bytes. The morph match and handheld goldens are over 16 KiB and carry a `sizeReason`.

`check-presets.mjs --goldens` on `dataviz`, `objects`, `transitions`, and `camera` reported nothing for these four, and `check-presets.mjs` passes. Two first drafts failed the mid-frame band, and both were redrawn:
- A radar that grew from the centre was already at its peak by the mid frame. The radar now moves between two series.
- A bottle that held one angle while pouring had already reached that angle by the mid frame. It now keeps tipping while it pours.

The visual review passed on rasters of frame 0, the mid frame, and the last frame, judged against [[sot:svgif/handoff]], plus in-between samples of the pour. A first handheld drift moved the view as far as an orbit, and it was halved.
