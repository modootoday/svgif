# Handoff H-309

## State

Started from `main`. `--goldens` reported 29 lines on the v1 `text` presets.

## Done

The mid-frame band now passes on every v1 text preset:

- **Slide-ins.** `text-callout`, `text-caption`, and `text-gradient-fill` slid in on `cubic-bezier(0.22, 1, 0.36, 1)`. They now use `cubic-bezier(0.4, 0.2, 0.45, 1)`, in the swap variants too.
- **Sweep.** `text-highlight-sweep` runs its bar and the ink clip over 20 frames on `cubic-bezier(0.45, 0, 0.55, 1)` instead of 18 frames on `cubic-bezier(0.65, 0, 0.35, 1)`.
- **Pops.** `text-badge` and `text-elastic-pop` reached their overshoot at frame 8. Their rise now runs on `cubic-bezier(0.55, 0, 0.6, 1)` to frame 22 for the badge and to frame 21 for the elastic pop, and the settle follows by frame 24. The poses are unchanged.
- **Staggers.** `text-split-lines`, `text-split-words`, and `text-split-chars` landed their first part by frame 5 to 10. Each part now travels about 20 frames, and the parts start 4, 2, and 2 frames apart. The `stagger` values and their bind anchors say the same.
- **Count rules.** `text-countdown` and `text-counter` change their digits in steps, so the digits' box could not track progress.
  - A timer bar under the countdown drains over its 90 frames.
  - A rule under the counter fills as the count runs.
  - Both use the ink colour, and `render-preset-index.mjs --contrast` passes on them.

`bun test` in `packages/svgif` passes all 721 tests. That includes the raster probes of `text-typewriter`, `text-counter`, and `text-karaoke` at their boundary frames.

The visual review rendered frame 0, the mid frame, and the last frame of each changed preset on the light stage, judged against [[sot:svgif/handoff]]. A first counter rule touched the digits and was moved down.

## Not done

- **Entrance gap.** 13 lines read `entrance mid frame is within 2 px of the last frame`: `block`, `caption`, `countdown`, `counter`, `elastic-pop`, `kinetic`, `marquee`, `mask-reveal`, `split-chars`, `split-lines`, `split-words`, `typewriter`, and `vertical-cjk`.
  - Each draws its text in `#1c1915`.
  - The golden check measures the entrance gap on the `#141414` stage and skips a pixel within 16 levels of the stage. `#1c1915` is at most 8 levels from it, so the text is not measured at all.
  - The Studio check needs 4.5:1 on white, which is why the ink is dark.
  - Fixing this means either a mid-tone ink such as `#726f68` for every text preset or measuring text presets on the light stage. It also changes the engine test's probe colours. That is the owner's call.
- **Missing binds.** `text-counter` `start`, `text-elastic-pop` `strength`, `text-marquee` `gap`, and `text-vertical-cjk` `tracking` need `unbound.json` entries.
