# Handoff H-291

## State

Started from `main`. Two `later` catalogue types reuse the v1 motion card: `motion.physics.inertia` and `motion.physics.followPath`.

## Done

- `motion-physics-inertia`: the card is pushed in from the left, then coasts and slows under friction (an exponential decay, constant 1.8 over 36 frames) until it stops at the centre. A small tilt from the push settles as it slows.
- `motion-physics-follow-path`: the card travels an S-curve across the stage over 48 frames and turns to face along the path.

Both keyed every 2 or 3 frames with linear segments, so the curve follows the sampled motion. No rotate step is over 30 degrees. The files are 4,248 and 9,239 bytes and the goldens 1,254 and 1,973 bytes.

`check-presets.mjs --goldens` on `motion` reported nothing for these two, and `check-presets.mjs` passes. A first inertia draft with a friction constant of 2.6 was past 75 percent of its travel at the mid frame; 1.8 puts it at about 71 percent. A first follow-path keyed every 3 frames turned 30.7 degrees in one step; every 2 frames keeps each step under 30.

The visual review passed on rasters of frame 0, the mid frame, and the last frame, judged against [[sot:svgif/handoff]], plus three in-between samples of the path.
