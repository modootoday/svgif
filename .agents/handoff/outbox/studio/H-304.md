# Handoff H-304

## State

Started from `main`. These are the last three `later` catalogue types: `text.handwritingReveal`, `text.pathFollow`, and `viz.mapSimple`. None of them needs an engine or schema change.

## Done

- `text-handwriting-reveal` (plays once): "hello" in a joined hand writes itself behind a pen.
  - The glyph-to-stroke preprocess the catalogue asks for is done at authoring time. The word is a centre line through Catmull-Rom control points, cut into thirty segments of equal arc length.
  - The segments appear in writing order, like the segments in `path-trim`.
  - The document is `kind: text` with the content `hello`.
- `text-path-follow` (plays once): "FOLLOW" rides in along an S-shaped path and stops with its lead letter four fifths of the way along. Each letter keeps its arc-length place on the path and turns with it, which is what `textPath` with a moving start offset would show. The letters use the block glyphs of the v1 text presets.
- `viz-map-simple` (plays once): a made-up island of five regions, drawn as a coastline outline.
  - The regions fill from west to east in five shades by value, and a pin moves to each region as it fills.
  - The map is invented, so it needs no map asset or projection.

The files are 9,603 to 34,985 bytes and the goldens 6,543 to 27,648 bytes. The two text presets carry a `sizeReason`.

`check-presets.mjs --goldens` on `text` and `dataviz` reported nothing for these three, and `check-presets.mjs` passes. Problems found while building them, all fixed:
- A `kind: text` document may not carry `copied.shape`, so the builder's shape was dropped.
- A first map drew the island as a filled shape and started the pin late. Its mid frame and last frame had the same extent, and the pin was outside the mid-frame band.

The visual review passed on rasters of frame 0, the mid frame, and the last frame, judged against [[sot:svgif/handoff]]. A first path follow ran its lead letters past the end of the path, so they piled up at the end.

With this batch all 56 `later` types are in the tree.
