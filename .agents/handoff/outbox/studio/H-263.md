Changes from H-262:

- A layer can keep its own fill. Such a layer is left out of the `color` bind: the shadow (`#6b6b6b`) and the white highlight band.
- The generator now also requires a visible layer, with opacity and scale above 0.2, at frame 0 and frame 28, so a one-shot effect such as the explosion still has a mark on the last frame.
- The scribble is an `attr.d` reveal. Points not drawn yet sit on the pen tip, so every key has the same commands. A first draft that grew it with `scaleX` read as a squeeze, not a line being drawn.
- The first shadow draft was black and did not show on the dark stage.
