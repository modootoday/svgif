# Handoff H-275

## State

Started from `main`. The `humans` category had no presets.

## Done

Twelve biped presets are in `presets/humans/`: the base biped (standing, breathing) and eleven gaits: walk, jog, run, sprint, sneak, tiptoe, limp, march, strut, skip, and hop.

They share a cut-out rig, as the catalogue maps it (`character.rig.cutout`: parent-child plus pivot, no bones, no IK in the runtime). The hierarchy is solved in the generator: each part is a flat layer keyed with its world pivot (translate) and world angle (rotate), so no nesting is needed. Back limbs are a darker shade. The shirt takes `color`.

Gait rules from the catalogue:

- A planted foot does not slide in world space. Each foot follows a world target that stays put through its stance and arcs forward through its swing, and a two-bone solve in the generator places the knee in front.
- Contact, down, passing, and up mirror on the opposite foot.

Notes for the preset check:

- An in-place walk paints a mirrored pose half a loop later, so the check saw no motion between the mid frame and the last frame. The gaits cross the stage over two cycles (three for tiptoe and hop), and the figure fades at both ends of the loop.
- Loop lengths are a whole number of tenths of a second (30, 45, 48, or 60 frames). The golden writes `dur` to four decimals, so a fractional duration such as 40 frames failed the strict sample at the loop length plus 0.1 seconds.

`check-presets.mjs --goldens` on the category reported nothing. The index check and `check-presets.mjs` pass. Coverage is 148 of 476, a gap of 328, counted on `main` plus this batch.

Raster review: in the first draft the knees bent backwards (the solve bent the wrong way for a figure facing right). All gaits now read.

Size: each part track is sampled every frame and keeps only the keys that linear playback needs, within 0.4 px for a translate and 1 degree for a rotate, and no rotate step over 30 degrees. Keys carry no easing, and each key list is on one line. The files are 12,606 to 39,971 bytes and the goldens 12,169 to 26,649 bytes, inside the byte budget in [[sot:svgif/preset-registry]].

The visual review passed on rasters of frame 0, the mid frame, and the last frame, judged against [[sot:svgif/handoff]].
