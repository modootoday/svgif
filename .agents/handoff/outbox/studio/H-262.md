# Handoff H-262

## State

Started from `main`. The `effects` category had no presets.

## Done

Twelve effect presets are in `presets/effects/`: confetti, sparkle, burst, impact, shockwave, speed lines, motion lines, dust, glitter, hearts emit, stars emit, and notes emit. Each is a looping 30-frame cycle of particle layers, with every layer bound to the `color` parameter.

How the particles are built:

- Each particle lives one cycle with a phase offset, and its opacity or scale is 0 at birth and death, so the jump back to its start is not seen.
- There is one key every 2 frames, which keeps every golden under 16 KB. A rotation follows a sine, so it has no jump at the wrap and no step over 30 degrees.
- The preset check reads the largest-moving track at frame 14 and wants it between 25 and 75 percent of its excursion. The generator shifts all phases together until that holds. Sparkle and glitter drift a little in y so a translate, not a scale pulse, is that track.
- Burst alternates its rays half a cycle apart, so the last frame still has a mark on the light stage.

`check-presets.mjs --goldens` on the category reported nothing. The index check and `check-presets.mjs` pass. Coverage is 148 of 476, a gap of 328, counted on `main` plus this batch.

Raster review at 0, 0.33, and 0.66 seconds. In the first draft the confetti and the dust fell in a diagonal line because x and phase rose together; their x order is now shuffled.

The visual review passed on rasters of frame 0, the mid frame, and the last frame, judged against [[sot:svgif/handoff]].
