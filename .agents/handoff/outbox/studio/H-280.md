# Handoff H-280

## State

Started from `main`. The `animals` category had no presets.

## Done

Sixteen quadruped presets are in `presets/animals/`: the base quadruped (standing), walk, amble, and trot, and twelve skins walking: dog, cat, horse, cow, deer, bear, pig, sheep, wolf, fox, lion, and tiger.

They share a cut-out quadruped rig built like the biped rig of pull request 505. The body is pitched between hip and shoulder; each leg is a two-bone solve onto a planted world foot (the stifle and hock bend back and the front knee forward); and neck, head, ears, and tail follow. The catalogue says the same rig takes a skin that changes proportion, ears, and coat, so a skin sets body and leg lengths, neck angle, ear type (point, round, flop, long, small), tail type, coat color, and optional spots, stripes, mane, horns, or antlers. Walk is a lateral sequence (hind, fore, other hind, other fore) and trot pairs the diagonals. The coat takes `color`.

Reach: a stride on a fixed crossing was out of reach for short legs and flipped the knee solve, so each crossing scales with the animal's leg length.

`check-presets.mjs --goldens` on the category reported nothing. The index check and `check-presets.mjs` pass. Coverage is 152 of 476, a gap of 324, counted on `main` plus this batch.

Raster review: every skin reads as its animal. The first sheep had dark spots and read as a cow; its wool tufts are now light grey.

Size: each part track is sampled every frame and keeps only the keys that linear playback needs, within 0.4 px for a translate and 1 degree for a rotate, and no rotate step over 30 degrees. Keys carry no easing, and each key list is on one line. The files are 15,933 to 42,767 bytes and the goldens 14,471 to 29,968 bytes, inside the byte budget in [[sot:svgif/preset-registry]].

The visual review passed on rasters of frame 0, the mid frame, and the last frame, judged against [[sot:svgif/handoff]].
