## Done

The mid-frame band and the entrance gap pass on all 15 dataviz presets. Motion that had already finished, or nearly finished, by the mid frame now runs through it. The shapes and the end frames do not change, except where listed below.

- `viz-bar`, `viz-bullet`, `viz-column`, `viz-funnel`, `viz-map-pin`, `viz-progress-bar`, `viz-sparkline`, `viz-stacked-bar`, and the `viz-timeline` track ran on `cubic-bezier(0.33, 1, 0.68, 1)`. That ease is about 87 percent done at the mid frame. They now use `cubic-bezier(0.4, 0.2, 0.45, 1)`, which is about 67 percent done, inside the 60 to 72 percent [[sot:svgif/preset-registry]] asks for.
- `viz-donut`, `viz-gauge`, `viz-heatmap`, and the `viz-scatter` dots are keyed densely. Each is resampled at its own key frames through a slower start, so its path shapes stay and only the timing moves. The gauge no longer trips the limb rule.
- `viz-bullet`, `viz-donut`, `viz-gauge`, and `viz-progress-bar` drew a full, filled background track under the growing value. That track hid the growth from the entrance-gap measure. The track is now a 2 px outline of the same shape and colour.
- `viz-pie` filled its first half in eight frames. The three slices are now one sweep in proportion to their angles: 180, 90, and 90 degrees. The sweep starts slowly, so the first slice is about half drawn at the mid frame.
- `viz-scatter`: the axis drew in three frames and was the main track. It now draws over the whole entrance.
- `viz-ticker`: the digits' box jumps with the digit count, so it could not track progress. A rule under the number now fills to the value's share of the scale as the count runs. The rule takes `color`.

The visual review passed on rasters of frame 0, the mid frame, and the last frame of all 15, judged against [[sot:svgif/handoff]]. A first ticker rule touched the digits and was moved down.
