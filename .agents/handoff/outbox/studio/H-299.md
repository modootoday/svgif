# Handoff H-299

## State

Started from `main`. Two `later` catalogue types use the Gaussian blur filter that `nature-fog` already uses: `filter.blur` and `trans.blurSwap`.

The engine applies a component's filter to its first layer only, so each preset draws everything in one layer.

## Done

- `filter-blur`: a card with three text lines cut out of it pulls out of focus and back, like a lens hunting, and drifts down while it is soft. `radius` scales the blur.
- `trans-blur-swap` (plays once, 24 frames): one card blurs up. Under the peak blur its colour and its window change to the incoming card's, and then the blur clears. `duration` retimes it.

The files are 2,252 to 2,875 bytes, and both goldens are under 2 KiB.

`check-presets.mjs --goldens` on `pathfx` and `transitions` reported nothing for these two, and `check-presets.mjs` passes.

The visual review rendered each time on its own page. On a sheet that holds several copies of one golden, every copy uses the first copy's filter, because the filter ids repeat. Rasters of frame 0, the mid frame, the last frame, and the blur peak were judged against [[sot:svgif/handoff]].

## Not done

The engine emits filter primitives in order, with no `in`, `result`, `type`, or `numOctaves`, and has no `feColorMatrix`. A trial `feTurbulence` replaced the shape with coloured noise and was not kept. These types need a filter graph in the engine first.
