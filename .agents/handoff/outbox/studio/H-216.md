## Done

Simple mode replaces the undo placeholder with Undo and Redo. Each edit a person can make is one step. That covers the words, adding a shape, a canvas move, a canvas resize, choosing a motion, replacing a motion, speed, delay, the length, and Loop. A move or a resize is one step for the whole drag. Undo restores the composition and the editor records, including `simpleMotions`, labels, texts, speed, and delay, so a later motion replace or length clamp still sees those records. Redo puts that edit back. A new edit after undo clears redo. Opening a project, importing a project, or replacing the composition clears both stacks. A button is disabled, and marked `aria-disabled`, when its stack is empty. The stacks keep at most 50 edits. Advanced edits that change this editor use the same stacks, and one finished artboard drag is one step there too. Play, pause, scrub, selection, pan, and zoom are not edits. While the person is not typing in a field, Ctrl or Cmd with Z undoes, Ctrl or Cmd with Shift and Z redoes, and Ctrl or Cmd with Y redoes.
