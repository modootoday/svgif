## Done

Six physics presets are in `presets/motion/`. The catalogue says physics is approximated with keys, and these are keys only:

- bounce: the card falls from the top, squashes on a ground line that puts its bottom at y 150, and bounces twice, each time lower.
- spring: the card slides in from the left and settles at the centre with three decaying swings.
- gravity: the card falls from the top with a growing speed and lands.
- pendulum: the hanging card from the loop pendulum swings 14, 12, 9, 6, and 3 degrees and stops.
- squash stretch: the card squashes and stretches on a fixed bottom edge, with `scaleX` set to the inverse of `scaleY` so the area stays.
- secondary: the card crosses the stage and an antenna layer on top of it lags, then swings past and settles after the card stops.

The motion preset id is the component id in lower case, because the id pattern has no capitals (`motion.physics.squashstretch`).
