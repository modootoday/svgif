## Done

Ten human presets are in `presets/humans/`:

- Travel on the rig: climb (hands and feet take ladder rungs while the climber rises), crawl (hands and knees across the ground), and swim (front-crawl arms turn over, legs flutter, across a water band). All three fade at both ends of the loop, like the gaits.
- Secondary motion: hair (a three-segment ponytail) and cloth (a two-segment cape). Each segment lags the one before it on a hopping figure, which the catalogue calls a manual offset ("child group with delayed keyframes").
- The cut-out rig itself, with each pivot shown as a white dot while the joints swing through their range.
- Face parts on a close-up head: blink (lids close twice), eye track (pupils follow a circling dot), brow (lift, then knit), and expression (neutral, happy, and surprised steps of the mouth and brows).
