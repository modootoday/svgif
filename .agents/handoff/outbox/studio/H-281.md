# Handoff H-281

## State

Started from `main`. Pull request 510 (H-280) adds the quadrupeds in the same folder, with other file names.

## Done

Thirteen looping animal presets are in `presets/animals/`:

- Swimmers: fish swim (head, body, and tail on a travelling wave, with bubbles), fish turn (swims, turns about with a horizontal flip, swims back), whale (the fluke beats up and down, as the catalogue's vertical tail wave asks, with a spout), dolphin (porpoises round an ellipse, over the surface and back under it, nose along its path), and jellyfish (the bell squeezes and the tentacles trail).
- Crawlers: snake (nine segments on a travelling wave across the ground), worm (an inchworm gathers and stretches), and snail (the body stretches, then the shell follows).
- Fliers: butterfly (wings out of step on a looping path), bee (blurred wings in a hover), and bat (membrane wings flap from the shoulders).
- Leggy: crab (scuttles side to side, legs working in turn) and insect (six legs in two alternating tripods, as the catalogue describes).

Travelling animals fade at both ends of the loop and draw a ground line, so the last frame still has a mark.

Notes for the preset check:

- The snake's wave is two whole waves over its crossing, so the wave has no seam at the wrap.
- The dolphin's heading turns once per loop.
- The snake and the dolphin carry `sizeReason`.

`check-presets.mjs --goldens` on the category reported nothing. The index check and `check-presets.mjs` pass. Coverage is 149 of 476, a gap of 327, counted on `main` plus this batch.

Raster review: in the first draft every motion was written for a 0 to 1 loop position, but the generator passes a frame index, so most animals barely moved and the travellers were invisible. Every layer now gets the loop position. The fish segments and the crab legs were also redrawn.

Size: each track is sampled every frame and keeps only the keys that linear playback needs, within 0.4 px for a translate and 1 degree for a rotate, and no rotate step over 30 degrees. Keys carry no easing, and each key list is on one line. The files are 4,396 to 18,824 bytes and the goldens 2,591 to 15,138 bytes, inside the byte budget in [[sot:svgif/preset-registry]].

The visual review passed on rasters of frame 0, the mid frame, and the last frame, judged against [[sot:svgif/handoff]].
