# Handoff H-317

## State

Started from `main` at `cc99f20`. The owner asked for a deep review of Studio's user journey, a visual review, a complete canonical design, and its implementation.

[[research:svgif/20261117015831]] followed one path through a real browser at 1440×900 in the light and dark themes, at 390×844 with touch, and at 360×640. It recorded ten findings. The largest:
- The ADR task *blank page, add a title and an icon, animate them in* could not be done in Simple mode.
- Download sat in the middle of the panel.
- Nothing named the selection.
- The Advanced top bar was cut off on a phone.

## Done

**Canonical design.** [[sot:svgif/studio-ui]] records the journey order, and its verification names the new checks.
- The order is Start, Words and color, Picture, Motion, and Length.
- Undo and Redo are pinned at the head of the panel, and Download at its foot.
- The record also covers the Blank start, the start pictures, the Picture controls, the motion list that stays open, the settled paused frame, the drawn Loop switch, and the Advanced More menu.

**Simple mode.**
- **Start.** Blank comes first. Each design shows a picture of itself on its first color, with its words drawn as a bar. A design's words are fitted inside its backdrop when it is applied, so Sale no longer sits off center.
- **Picture.** The block names the selection. It has Add words, Add shape, Add icon (a star), and Remove. Delete or Backspace also removes. Added words are real text and keep their center when the headline changes.
- **Motion.** The note names the element the entrance goes on. The list stays open after a choice, with the current motion marked, and its button reads Done while it is open. Escape closes the list.
- **Paused frame.** Paused, the picture shows the last frame, so an element is never hidden behind its entrance. Play starts at the beginning.
- **Clock.** It reads the time and the length. A phone shows only the time.
- **Loop and messages.** Loop is a drawn switch, and its checkbox keeps the 44 px hit area. The Save message and the motion message are plain sentences.

**Advanced.**
- On a phone, Open, Save, and Export sit behind More, so no control is cut off.
- Library rows show the preset name in words, with its category below it.
- The inspector rounds numbers to two decimals and heads the selection with its label instead of its id.

**More starts.** Launch and Thank you are new data files under `apps/studio/templates/`, after Sale. Each has four named colors and an essential mark.
- `apps/studio/test/start-designs.test.ts` holds every listed design to these rules:
  - Each color keeps the words and the mark at 4.5:1 or better.
  - The mark sits inside the card.
  - The words keep at least half the card's width beside the mark.
  - Each color repaints the words.
- No Studio code names the new files.

**Checks.**
- `studio-shell.sh` runs the add task at 1440×900 with a mouse and at 390×844 with touch. It requires 6 actions or fewer, real text, the marked motion, the named selection, and a working Remove.
- `studio-shell.sh` also checks that no Advanced top bar control is cut off at the four viewports.
- `apps/studio/test/simple-add.test.ts` covers adding, removing, Blank, the star, and the start pictures.

Measured:
- The add task is 6 actions at 1440×900, 390×844, and 360×640.
- The Advanced top bar has `scrollWidth` equal to `clientWidth` at all four sizes.
- `bun test` in `apps/studio` passes 83 tests.
- `npm run typecheck` exits 0.
