# Handoff H-292

## State

Started from `main`. Two `later` catalogue types reuse the v1 projected 3D generator: `prim3d.torus` and `prim3d.extrude`.

## Done

- `prim3d-torus`: a torus keeps its outline and shading as it spins about the vertical axis, like the v1 sphere and capsule. It is a still outline with the hole cut out by reversed winding, a still shadow, and two tube rings in the light marker colour that turn so the full turn reads. Each ring has 8 faces.
- `prim3d-extrude`: a convex pentagon profile pushed through depth and turned a full revolution. The front cap is the marker, so the turn reads, and every face fades as it turns away, like the v1 cylinder and cone.

The files are 15,886 and 37,917 bytes and the goldens 12,079 and 28,150 bytes, inside the byte budget in [[sot:svgif/preset-registry]]. A first torus with 10 faces per ring had a 36.6 KB golden; 8 faces fit.

`check-presets.mjs --goldens` on `prim3d` reported nothing, and `check-presets.mjs` passes.

The visual review passed on rasters of frame 0, the mid frame, and the last frame, judged against [[sot:svgif/handoff]], plus three in-between samples of each spin.
