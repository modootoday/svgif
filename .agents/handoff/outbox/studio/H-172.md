## Done

Camera parameters that follow the new kinds now carry a bind. `at` is the declared number. No gain, pixel constant, or angle ratio is stored on the bind. `camera-zoom` `scale` is unchanged.

`distance` on every camera preset, and `margin` on `camera-frame`, use `inverse-scale` of `attr.d`. The layers are the faces the declared pose bakes: `subject-f0`, `subject-f2`, `subject-f4`, `ground-f0`, `ground-f2`, and `ground-f4`.

`x` uses `projected-offset` of `attr.d` on `camera-cut`, `camera-dolly`, `camera-frame`, `camera-ken-burns`, `camera-pan`, `camera-parallax`, and `camera-zoom`. `y` uses the same kind on those presets and on `camera-orbit`. `axis` is `x` or `y`. The layers are the same declared faces. `camera-shake` has neither parameter.

`latitude` and `azimuth` use `reproject` of `attr.d` on every camera preset. The layers are `subject-f0` through `subject-f5` and `ground-f0` through `ground-f5`.

Left unbound, as this package requires:

- `camera-cut` `view`
- `camera-zoom` `center`
- `camera-ken-burns` `start` and `endFrame`
- `camera-parallax` `depthRatio`
- `camera-shake` `strength`
- `camera-shake` `seed`, which stays the only `inexpressible` entry and was not given a bind

At each declared value the render matches the same preset with the binds removed. Committed golden files were not rewritten. `camera-cut` and `camera-frame` already match the generator. The other seven camera goldens already differ from the generator with the binds removed. Those files stay as they are.

Each new bind was sampled on its own, with the other binds removed, at frame 0, the quarter frame, the mid frame, and the last frame. The targeted nodes changed. Pixels outside them did not. Doubling `distance` or `margin` halves each baked face, and halving either value doubles the face. A projected offset follows the compiler gain with the opposite sign. The shift is not the raw parameter delta. On `camera-parallax` that gain still depends on subject depth. A reproject, with the other binds removed, matches the compiler camera. A `+0.2` step keeps the declared faces. The `+24` latitude step drops `subject-f0`, `subject-f4`, `ground-f0`, and `ground-f4` and adds `subject-f1`, `subject-f5`, `ground-f1`, and `ground-f5`, except on `camera-parallax`, where the declared faces stay. The `+24` azimuth step drops `subject-f4` and `ground-f4` and adds `subject-f5` and `ground-f5`, except on `camera-orbit`, which keeps `subject-f4` and `ground-f4` and adds `subject-f5` and `ground-f5`. The reproject layers name the faces those probes drop and the faces they add.

With the other binds left in place, that `+24` step throws `bind names a missing node` whenever the probe removes a face that `inverse-scale`, `projected-offset`, or the existing `camera-zoom` `scale` bind still names. `camera-orbit` azimuth and `camera-parallax` latitude still render, because those probes do not remove such a face. This package does not edit the checker.
