`retime` multiplies key frames by `value / at`. That lengthens `seconds`, `alignTime`, and `stagger` when the value grows. `speed` is not bound: the same multiply would slow a larger speed, which is the wrong direction.

### Swap values

`text-callout` `target` covers `center`, `left`, and `right`. `bubble` and `label` already share one `transform.translateX`, so one key list is the motion of both. `center` is the authored pair, frame 0 at 180 and frame 24 at 0. `left` ends at -64. `right` ends at 64.

`text-caption` `position` covers `bottom`, `center`, and `top`. `bottom` is the authored pair, frame 0 at 80 and frame 24 at 0. `center` ends at -42, which puts the glyph-box center on the frame center. `top` ends at -82, which puts the glyph top at y 18.

`text-mask-reveal` `direction` covers `right` and `left`. `right` is the authored clip: closed at x 94, then open from x 90 to 230. `left` is closed at x 230 and opens to the same end rect.

`text-typewriter` `cursor` covers `bar` and `block`. `bar` is the authored stem, x 226–228 and y 66–116. `block` is the same vertical span from x 206 to 228.
