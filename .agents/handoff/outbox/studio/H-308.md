# Handoff H-308

## State

Started from `main`. `--goldens` reported 31 lines on the v1 `nature` presets.

## Done

- **Loop start phase.** `nature-cloud-drift`, `fog`, `moon`, `grass-sway`, `tree-sway`, and `wind-implied` are ping-pong loops whose peak sat on the mid frame, 92 to 98 percent of travel. Each loop now starts 15 percent into its cycle. Every track shifts by the same number of frames, so the motion is the same; only frame 0 moves. Mid-frame progress is now 52 to 65 percent.
- **Growth through the mid frame.** `flower-bloom` and `seed-sprout` grew over frames 0 to 16 and now grow over 0 to 23 on `cubic-bezier(0.4, 0.2, 0.45, 1)`. `rain-splash` and `water-splash` now throw their drops over 0 to 23 and fade from 17.
- **Whole beats.** `rain-fall`, `star-twinkle`, `sun-pulse`, `water-stream`, and `water-wave` loop at 75 bpm on 23 frames, which is 0.96 beats. They now loop on 24 frames, one beat, so their wraps match frame 0.
  - The `direction` swap variants of the stream and the wave carry their own keys, and those moved too.
  - `water-wave` drifted its 88 px period with a seam that did not hold at 24 frames, so it loops over 48 frames, two beats.
  - `snow-fall` went from 59 to 60 frames, three beats at 90 bpm.
- **A mark that stays.** `lightning`, `smoke-puff`, `water-bubble`, `water-ripple`, `rain-splash`, and `water-splash` ended on an empty frame, so nothing was left on the light stage. Each now keeps a context mark:
  - a storm cloud the bolt leaves from
  - a chimney under the puff
  - the water surface the bubble pops at
  - a water line under the ripple and the splashes
  The documents became layered, and the old shape is the first layer, so the existing tracks and binds still address it.
- **`nature-rainbow`.** It only brightened from 0.85 to 1, so its mid frame and last frame measured the same. It is now a loop on one beat at 90 bpm, 20 frames, that shimmers to full and settles.

The visual review rendered frame 0, the mid frame, and the last frame of every preset whose drawing changed, each time on its own page, and judged them against [[sot:svgif/handoff]].
