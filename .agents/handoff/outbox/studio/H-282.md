## Done

Twelve animal presets are in `presets/animals/`:

- Birds: fly, glide, hover, walk, peck, take off, and land. The catalogue maps birds to torso and wing hierarchy, upper arm first: each wing is an upper part that turns first and a hand part that lags and folds on the upstroke. The downstroke makes lift (the body rises) and the upstroke folds.
- Hoppers: rabbit and kangaroo (crouch, push, arc, land, with the kangaroo's tail balancing) and frog (legs stretch in the air).
- Penguin waddle (rocks side to side on short steps, flippers out) and turtle walk (slow paddling legs under a steady shell, a nodding head).

Travelling animals fade at both ends of the loop and draw a ground line.

Notes for the preset check:

- Wings and legs turn fast, so this batch is sampled every frame before the keys are thinned.
- Take off and land keyed a wing that jumped between folded and flapping at the loop wrap. Take off now flaps from its first frame over two whole beats, so it ends where it began. Land ends half folded and, while faded out, eases back to its first pose, so the keys run through the wrap.

`check-presets.mjs --goldens` on the category reported nothing. The index check and `check-presets.mjs` pass. Coverage is 148 of 476, a gap of 328, counted on `main` plus this batch.

Raster review: every animal reads. The penguin's flippers and the gliding bird's wings are the roughest.

Size: each track is sampled every frame and keeps only the keys that linear playback needs, within 0.4 px for a translate and 1 degree for a rotate, and no rotate step over 30 degrees. Keys carry no easing, and each key list is on one line. The files are 10,553 to 20,361 bytes and the goldens 8,156 to 14,438 bytes, inside the byte budget in [[sot:svgif/preset-registry]].

The visual review passed on rasters of frame 0, the mid frame, and the last frame, judged against [[sot:svgif/handoff]].
