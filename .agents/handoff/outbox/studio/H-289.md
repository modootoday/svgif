# Handoff H-289

## State

Started from `main`. Every v1 catalogue type has a preset (476 of 476). The catalogue records 56 `later` types; four of them reuse the v1 quadruped rig: `character.quadruped.pace`, `character.quadruped.canter`, `character.quadruped.gallop`, and `character.quadruped.elephant`.

## Done

Four presets in `presets/animals/` on the quadruped cut-out rig, crossing the stage in two cycles and fading at both ends, like the v1 gaits:

- `pace`: the two legs on one side move together.
- `canter`: three beats, a hind leg, then the diagonal pair, then the leading fore leg.
- `gallop`: four beats with a short stance, so there is a moment of suspension after the leading fore leg.
- `elephant`: a walk with a heavy grey body, big flop ears, a trunk that hangs from the snout, and tusks. The rig gained a `big` ear shape and an optional trunk part.

Each part track is sampled every frame and keeps only the keys that linear playback needs, with no rotate step over 30 degrees. Loop lengths are 48, 60, or 72 frames. The files are 34,845 to 40,194 bytes and the goldens 25,031 to 28,709 bytes, inside the byte budget in [[sot:svgif/preset-registry]].

`check-presets.mjs --goldens` on the category reported nothing, and `check-presets.mjs` passes.

The visual review passed on rasters of frame 0, the mid frame, and the last frame, judged against [[sot:svgif/handoff]], plus three in-between samples of each gait. A first elephant draft had a short trunk that read as part of the head; a longer, wider trunk fixed it. A first gallop at 15 frames a cycle turned a leg 34 degrees in one frame; 24 frames a cycle keeps every step under 30.
