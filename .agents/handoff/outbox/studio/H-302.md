# Handoff H-302

## State

Started from `main`. Two `later` catalogue types reuse v1 generators:
- `character.biped.talk` uses the face parts of the v1 face presets.
- `character.dragon.fly` uses the v1 creature parts with a body, a neck, a tail, and wings.

## Done

- `character-biped-talk`: a standing figure speaks while one forearm gestures. The mouth steps through the ten shapes the catalogue names: rest, A/I, E, O, U, C-class, F/V, L, M/B/P, and W/Q. Each shape sets the mouth's width and height, and whether the teeth or the tongue show. The skin takes `color`.
- `character-dragon-fly`: a dragon hovers and drifts forward and back. Its wings beat twice a loop, and the body rises on each downstroke while the neck and tail ripple.
  - Seen from the side, each beat is the wing folding from above the shoulder to below it.
  - Each neck and tail segment names the one before it as its `parent`, so the attachment check follows the chain. The body takes `color`.

The files are 21,774 and 29,072 bytes and the goldens 17,350 and 21,072 bytes. Both carry a `sizeReason` for keys on every frame.

`check-presets.mjs --goldens` on `humans` and `creatures` reported nothing for these two, and `check-presets.mjs` passes. A first dragon failed three checks, all fixed:
- Its eye, dark wing, and shadow were outside the creature luminance band. The colours were changed and the shadow was removed.
- Its chain parts had no parent, so the check measured them against the body.
- Every motion ran twice a loop, so no offset put the main track in the mid-frame band. The slow drift fixed this.

The visual review passed on rasters of frame 0, the mid frame, and the last frame, judged against [[sot:svgif/handoff]], plus in-between samples. A first dragon rotated its wings forward over its head and hid it.
