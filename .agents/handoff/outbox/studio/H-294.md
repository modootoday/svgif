# Handoff H-294

## State

Started from `main`. Three `later` catalogue types reuse the v1 face and figure parts: `character.face.viseme`, `character.rig.frameSwap`, and `character.rig.morph`.

A scan of every preset on `main` for tracks that never change found the four v1 face presets from H-279 static: `character-face-blink`, `character-face-eye-track`, `character-face-brow`, and `character-face-expression`. Their generator wrote each motion for a position from 0 to 1 but was called with a frame index, so every sine landed on a whole turn and every key held one value, apart from the small bump that keeps a track alive. The only other static preset is `bg-solid`, which is still by design.

## Done

- The four v1 face presets are regenerated with the frame index turned into a loop position. The blink closes the lids, the eye-track pupils follow the dot, the brows lift and knit, and the expression changes.
- `character-face-viseme`: the v1 face's mouth steps through the shapes speech makes, rest, A, E, O, U, and M, holding each and easing into the next. The teeth show on E.
- `character-rig-frame-swap`: four drawings of a jumping jack shown one after another, like a flipbook, with a small bounce.
- `character-rig-morph`: one outline moves between a stand, a crouch, and a reach by moving its points. It is keyed every frame so no point swings more than 30 degrees about the first between keys.

All files are under 12.3 KB and all goldens under 9 KB.

`check-presets.mjs --goldens` on `humans` reported nothing, and `check-presets.mjs` passes.

The visual review passed on rasters of frame 0, the mid frame, and the last frame, judged against [[sot:svgif/handoff]], plus three in-between samples of every face and the flipbook. The first viseme and flipbook drafts were static for the same reason as the v1 faces. The review of H-279 passed those four faces because their frames looked plausible; it did not compare frames for change.
