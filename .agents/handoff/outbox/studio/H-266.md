## Done

Thirteen looping icon presets are in `presets/icons/`: spinner, gear spin, menu morph, bell ring, play pause, toggle, switch, alert pulse, Wi-Fi bars, battery fill, volume wave, clock tick, and chevron nav. They use the mitred outlines from H-265. Layers in the bound color take `color`; the white knobs and the grey off-track keep their own fill.

What the preset check needed:

- A shape with rotational symmetry paints the same centroid and spread at every angle, so the check sees no motion between the mid frame and the last frame. The gear has one curved slot and turns a full 360 degrees. The clock's second hand is wider and has a tip dot.
- Rotate keys stay at or under 30 degrees per step (bell ring), and an `attr.d` morph adds an in-between key where vertices would turn more than 30 degrees (menu morph on its way back).
- Opacity-only icons (Wi-Fi bars) carry a 0.01 translate nudge so a transform track sets the mid-frame band. The volume arcs peak after frame 14.

`check-presets.mjs --goldens` on the category reported nothing for the thirteen. It still reports `speed has no bind` for `icon-checkbox-draw` on `main`, which this branch does not touch. The index check and `check-presets.mjs` pass. Coverage is 149 of 476, a gap of 327, counted on `main` plus this batch.

The visual review passed on rasters of frame 0, the mid frame, and the last frame, judged against [[sot:svgif/handoff]] and the approved style in [[sot:svgif/preset-registry]].
