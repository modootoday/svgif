# Handoff H-298

## State

Started from `main`. Five `later` catalogue types reuse the v1 particle generator: `path.morph`, `path.follow`, `path.trim`, `path.dashMarch`, and `mask.clockWipe`.

The engine has no dash offset property, so `path.trim` and `path.dashMarch` do not use one:
- `path-trim` is a stroke cut into thirty-two short segments that appear in order behind a pen.
- `path-dash-march` keeps its dashes in place and moves an on and off pattern along them.

`path-follow` keys position and rotation instead of an offset path. `mask-clock-wipe` grows a sector outline instead of animating a clip.

## Done

- `path-morph`: one outline moves its 40 points from a circle to a five-point star to a square and back. The shape takes `color`.
- `path-follow`: a paper plane flies around a looping track drawn under it and turns with the curve.
- `path-trim` (plays once): a looping, handwriting-like stroke draws itself behind a pen.
- `path-dash-march`: twenty-eight dashes around a selection box run an on and off pattern, and the four corner handles pulse.
- `mask-clock-wipe` (plays once): a hand sweeps once around a dial and reveals a larger new face behind it.

The files are 5,453 to 36,609 bytes and the goldens 3,760 to 28,604 bytes. The trim and dash march goldens are over 16 KiB and carry a `sizeReason`.

`check-presets.mjs --goldens` on `pathfx` reported nothing for these five, and `check-presets.mjs` passes. Problems found while building them, all fixed:
- A trim that grew one outline moved its far points too far between keys for the limb rule.
- Dashes that moved along the box changed their position from start to end, so the checker read the preset as an entrance.
- The clock wipe's mid frame and last frame had the same extent until the new face became larger than the old one.

The visual review passed on rasters of frame 0, the mid frame, and the last frame, judged against [[sot:svgif/handoff]], plus in-between samples. The trim's last segment never got an opacity track, so it showed from the first frame. It now has one.
