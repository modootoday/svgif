# Handoff H-264

## State

Started from `main`. The `prim3d` category had no presets, and the preset check has no 3D path for it.

## Done

Ten presets are in `presets/prim3d/`: box, sphere, cylinder, cone, pyramid, capsule, plane, low-poly rock, low-poly house, and turntable. Each is a flat-shaded convex mesh projected with a fixed camera (24 degree pitch, perspective) into one `attr.d` track per face.

How the meshes are built:

- Every visible face is two layers: a black base and a face in the bound color whose opacity carries the shading. A face fades out as it turns away. Faces of a convex mesh never overlap, so the layer order does not matter. The house draws its roof after its walls.
- The box, pyramid, and plane turn one symmetry period (90 degrees) in 30 frames, so the loop wraps without a seam. The rock and the house sway.
- The cylinder, cone, and turntable turn a full 360 degrees over 60 frames, with a light `#f0d2b0` marker band so the turn reads. A uniform turn puts every face at its farthest point at the mid frame, outside the 25 to 75 percent band, so the speed varies smoothly: slower in the first half and faster in the second, with the same speed at the wrap.
- A sphere and a capsule keep the same outline and shading as they spin about the vertical axis. Each is a still outline in the bound color (two arcs for the sphere, the hull of a fine mesh for the capsule), a still shadow at 0.3 opacity, and two marker bands that turn.
- The preset check fails an `attr.d` step that turns a vertex more than 30 degrees about vertex 0. The generator runs the same test per face and adds keys only on the face that needs them. A path key inside a span where the face is hidden is dropped when the step across it still passes that test. An opacity key between two equal neighbours is dropped. Path points are whole pixels.

Sizes, against the byte budget in [[sot:svgif/preset-registry]] (64 KiB per file, 32 KiB per golden): the largest file is the turntable at 35,787 bytes and the largest golden is the turntable at 28,223 bytes. The category was 1.4 MB of JSON and 0.6 MB of goldens; it is now 332 KB in all.

`check-presets.mjs --goldens` on the category reported nothing. The index check and `check-presets.mjs` pass.

The visual review passed on rasters of frame 0, the mid frame, and the last frame, judged against [[sot:svgif/handoff]]. A 6 by 3 faceted sphere read as a gem and a faceted capsule as a barrel; the still outlines fixed both.
