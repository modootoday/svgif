# Handoff H-301

## State

Started from `main`. Three `later` catalogue types reuse the v1 animal generator: `character.spider.octoped`, `character.octopus`, and `character.monkey.brachiate`.

The spider follows `character-insect-hexapod`, and the octopus follows `character-jellyfish-pulse`.

## Done

- `character-spider-octoped`: a spider, seen from above, walks along a silk line. Its eight legs are bent at the knee and step in two alternating sets of four. The body takes `color`.
- `character-octopus`: the mantle squeezes and the body jets up. The eight arms, mirrored on the two sides, trail and curl, then spread as it sinks back.
- `character-monkey-brachiate`: a monkey swings hand over hand along a bar, over a two-second loop.
  - The holding hand stays on its rung while the shoulder swings 40 degrees each way. The rung spacing is twice the arm length times the sine of that angle, so the free hand reaches over the top and lands exactly on the next rung.
  - The body, legs, and tail lean forward during each swing and are upright again at each catch.

The files are 20,717 to 29,212 bytes and the goldens 16,371 to 19,322 bytes. All three carry a `sizeReason` for keys on every frame.

`check-presets.mjs --goldens` on `animals` reported nothing for these three, and `check-presets.mjs` passes. Problems found while building them, all fixed:
- A first monkey tied its lean to the swing angle, which jumps at each catch, so its tail and legs turned more than 30 degrees between keys.
- A first spider vanished from a light stage on the last frame. The silk line now stays.

The visual review passed on rasters of frame 0, the mid frame, and the last frame, judged against [[sot:svgif/handoff]], plus in-between samples. It found two problems, both fixed:
- The octopus arms all curled to one side.
- The spider's abdomen hid its back legs.
