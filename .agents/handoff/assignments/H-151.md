For `calcMode="discrete"`, an interior `keyTime` is `((n - 0.5) / fps) / D`. `n` is that key's frame, `fps` is the timeline rate, and `D` is the `dur` this compiler emits for that animation after dur rounding. The first key stays `0`. The last key stays `1`. A key that is not discrete keeps its current `keyTime`.

One unit in the sixth decimal is not this rule. Dur is rounded up, so `0.749999` can still land on or after the seek. https://github.com/modootoday/svgif/pull/275 stays open until the half-frame form is what the branch emits. Karaoke frame 8 and counter frame 17 still fail that one-unit bias.
