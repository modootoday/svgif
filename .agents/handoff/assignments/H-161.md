The bind names layer ids that exist in that preset and one kind: `replace`, `scale`, `offset`, `swap`, `repeat`, or `retime`. `color` replaces the fill of the named layers. `expression` swaps the face-part keys. `speed` or `cycle` retimes the duration. `count` or `density` sets the repeat count. `angle` or `direction` changes rotation or the travel vector. `size` or `radius` scales the named geometry. Use the kind that matches the parameter. Do not hash a value. Do not add an offset to the first number of the first path. Do not move the stage.

At the declared default value the golden bytes stay identical. A changed value changes the rendered pixels of the targeted nodes and leaves every other node unchanged.
