Add three kinds. Each is data the generator applies. No hash, no per-preset branch, and no hand-tuned pixel constant stored on the bind.

An inverse scale makes the face size follow `at / value`. At the declared value the factor is 1, so the default bytes stay identical. A larger value makes the subject smaller. This is the kind for `distance` and for `camera-frame` `margin`. Ordinary `scale` makes the face larger and is the wrong kind. Do not encode the inverse by a negative scale or by changing what the parameter means.

A projected offset moves the picture opposite the camera shift. The gain comes from the compiler camera math in `cameraPose` and `shiftEyeByPixels`. Without `depthRatio`, the pose reader shifts the eye by `(value - rest) * subjectDepth` along camera right and up, the depth cancels, and the screen gain is `height / (2 * tan(fovY / 2))` pixels per unit. With `depthRatio`, the strafe is the translate itself, so the gain still depends on depth. The sign is opposite in both cases. `offset` adds `value - at` pixels with the same sign and no gain, so it is the wrong kind. Do not write a constant gain into the bind.

A re-projection of the baked faces is the kind for `latitude` and `azimuth`. It uses the same camera the compiler uses. Scaling the existing outline by the ratio of the two angles is the wrong kind. Latitude is negative on these presets, so a larger value must not shrink or flip the rest outline. The checker's alternate value stays the `+24` H-166 left. That probe drops `subject-f4` and `ground-f4` and adds `subject-f5` and `ground-f5`. Do not shrink the probe so the rest face ids survive. Do not name only the faces that survive `+24`. The kind is honest when the picture matches the compiler camera at the probed angle, and pixels outside the projected subject and ground stay put. The stage is not a target. Do not add a layer so the check has a target.

The H-167 rules stay. A `swap` is valid only when the parameter declares `values` and the variants cover exactly that set, including the default. A variant `layer` has to be a bind layer. These three kinds are not swaps. A continuous angle or position does not declare a made-up set. Do not hash a value into an offset. Do not change the first number of the first path. Do not move the stage. `schema/fixtures/preset/unbound.json` exempts a listed free string. A listed number, a listed point, and a declared enumeration still need a bind when a kind can express that parameter. A number that no kind can follow in direction and magnitude may be listed, and `--goldens` does not fail that listed number. A listed number that an existing kind or one of these three kinds can express still fails. That exemption is not a way to skip `distance`, `margin`, `x`, `y`, `latitude`, or `azimuth`.
