- `character.biped` poses are contact, down, passing, and up. The opposite foot mirrors them. Frame tables, 1-based, and the next frame is not rendered: 12 fps contact 1 and 7, down 3 and 9, passing 4 and 10, up 6 and 12, next 13. 24 fps contact 1 and 13, down 4 and 16, passing 7 and 19, up 10 and 22, next 25. 30 fps contact 1 and 16, down 5 and 20, passing 9 and 24, up 13 and 28, next 31. At 24 fps the poses are 3 frames apart.
- Anti-foot-slide: `groundSpeed = stride / stepTime`. For a biped, `stepTime = cycle / 2`. A planted foot stays fixed in world space until the heel lifts. Root travel over one step equals the stride. Example: 24 fps, 24-frame cycle, stride 80 px, step 0.5 s, ground speed 160 px per second.
- Parts and pivots are the lists in the extract. Parameters: `gait`, `cycleMs`, `stridePx`, `bouncePx`, `armSwingDeg`, `weight`, `facing`, `inPlace`, `loop`.
- `character.quadruped` gaits: walk, amble, trot, pace, canter, gallop. Walk, amble, and trot are v1. Pace, canter, and gallop are later. Skins include dog, cat, and horse. A walk keeps two or three feet down. A trot is a two-beat diagonal. A gallop adds a suspension.
- Bird flight: the downstroke makes lift and thrust. The upstroke folds and reduces area. Fish: a sine wave that grows from head to tail. Snake: a lateral wave. Insect: alternating tripod, left-front with right-middle and left-hind, against right-front with left-middle and right-hind.
- Authoring methods: cutout rig is the v1 standard (parent-child groups and pivot rotation). Frame swap and path morph are later. Path morph interpolates only when command count, kinds, and order match. Skeletal IK is out of scope.
- Mouth shapes, later: rest, A/I, E, O, U, C-class, F/V, L, M/B/P, W/Q.
