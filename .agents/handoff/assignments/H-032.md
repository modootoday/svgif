The record is the editor model:

- The artboard is where a person builds an animated SVG.
- The library holds shapes, text, icons and stickers, 3D primitives, and motion presets. Adding a shape, text, icon, sticker, or 3D primitive creates a scene node on the artboard.
- Layers are those scene nodes. Reordering a layer reorders the node.
- The property controls follow the selected node.
- Each node has its own timeline. An edit on that timeline changes that node's tracks.
- A motion preset is a reusable animation block applied to one node. Applying it writes the block onto that node's tracks. It does not add a second timeline.
- A template is a starting point. It is not the default tab. The artboard is the default surface.

Copy the shell into [[sot:svgif/studio-ui]] under its own heading. Do not round the sizes.

The shell is a full-viewport grid of `100dvh` with `overflow: hidden`. There is no page-level horizontal or vertical scroll at any viewport. Scroll and pan happen only inside panels and in the artboard transform. After a panel collapses or a sash moves, the artboard fits.

Desktop, 1100px and wider: top bar 48; rail 48 plus library 280, resizing from 240 to 400 and collapsing to 0; artboard `minmax(0, 1fr)`; inspector 300, resizing from 260 to 400 and collapsing; per-layer timeline 168, minimum 120, maximum the smaller of 360px and `40dvh`, collapsing to a transport of 48; resizable sashes.

Tablet, 768px through 1099px: rail plus artboard; one overlay of 320, either the library or the inspector; timeline 120 with maximum `32dvh`, or the transport of 48.

Phone, below 768px: top bar 48 plus the top safe area; artboard `1fr`; a bottom sheet with snaps at `40dvh`, `70dvh`, and `90dvh`; mode tabs Preview, Library, Inspect, and Timeline, height 56 plus the bottom safe area.

Interactions: Space is play and pause. Pan with H, the middle mouse button, or two fingers. A layer row and a timeline row share one id, and selection shows that node in the inspector. The timeline filter is selected layer only. Templates are a starting point, not the default tab.
