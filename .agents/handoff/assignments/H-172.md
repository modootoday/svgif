## Next

Edit only `presets/camera/`. Bind the parameters below. Each kind follows the parameter's sign and magnitude. Do not store a hand-tuned gain, pixel constant, or angle ratio on the bind. At the declared value the golden bytes stay identical.

`inverse-scale` on `distance` for every camera preset, and on `camera-frame` `margin`. `property` is `attr.d`. `at` is the declared number. The factor is `at / value`. A larger value shrinks the faces. Doubling the value halves the size. Ordinary `scale` grows them and is the wrong kind. Name the subject and ground faces the declared pose bakes. Do not name a face that pose does not have.

`projected-offset` on `x` and `y`. `x` is on `camera-cut`, `camera-dolly`, `camera-frame`, `camera-ken-burns`, `camera-pan`, `camera-parallax`, and `camera-zoom`. `y` is on those presets and on `camera-orbit`. `camera-shake` has neither. `property` is `attr.d`. `at` is the declared number. `axis` is `x` or `y`. The picture moves opposite the camera shift by the gain `cameraPose` derives. Without `depthRatio` that gain is `height / (2 * tan(fovY / 2))` pixels per unit. On `camera-parallax` the strafe is the translate itself, so the gain is that quantity divided by subject depth. The shift is not `value - at` pixels. Do not write the gain into the bind. Name the faces the declared pose bakes.

`reproject` on `latitude` and `azimuth` for every camera preset. `property` is `attr.d`. `at` is the declared angle. Name `subject-f0` through `subject-f5` and `ground-f0` through `ground-f5`. The `+24` probe drops `subject-f4` and `ground-f4` and adds `subject-f5` and `ground-f5`. The bind names both the faces it drops and the faces it adds. The picture matches the compiler camera at the new angle. A larger latitude does not shrink the outline by the angle ratio. A plain path scale is the wrong kind. Pixels outside the projected subject and ground stay put. The stage is not a target.

Leave `camera-zoom` `scale` as it is. Do not bind `camera-shake` `seed`. It is the only `inexpressible` entry. Do not bind `start`, `endFrame`, `depthRatio`, `strength`, `view`, or `center` in this package. Do not add an `inexpressible` reason. Do not edit the checker, the schema, `unbound.json`, the org chart, the plan, or the registry. Do not name another product. Do not dispatch the release workflow. Open the pull request against `main`. `spec check` is green.
