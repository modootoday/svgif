Nineteen parameters follow the value. `replace` sets colors. `scale` sets size, weight, and travel. `offset` sets leading. `retime` sets seconds, alignTime, and stagger. Four swaps cover a closed set: `text-callout` `target` is `center`, `left`, and `right` on `bubble` and `label`, which share one `transform.translateX` on `main`, so one key list is both layers. `text-caption` `position` is `bottom`, `center`, and `top`. `text-mask-reveal` `direction` is `right` and `left`. `text-typewriter` `cursor` is `bar` and `block`. The committed text goldens already differ from the generator, including mid-frame band failures, with these binds removed. This package did not rewrite them.
